Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

WallTok (walltok.com) helps sellers in Nepal make TikTok content for their products and run a simple online shop. This page explains what information we keep and why.

If you are a seller

  • Google sign-in: when you make your shop link live, you sign in with Google. We receive your name, email address and Google account ID. We use them only to sign you in and keep your shop private to you. We do not receive your Google password, contacts, files or anything else.
  • Your shop: we store the shop name, WhatsApp number, location, product photos, product details, orders you record and view counts, so you can use them on any phone.
  • Before you sign in: everything you make is saved only in your phone's browser and is not sent to us.

If you are a customer

  • You can browse a shop without an account. When you open a product we count one view for that product, without storing your name, phone number or IP address.
  • Orders happen on WhatsApp, directly between you and the seller. WallTok does not see your WhatsApp messages.
  • If a seller records your order in WallTok (for example your name and delivery area), that information belongs to the seller and is visible only to them.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information, show ads based on it, or share it with anyone, except the services that run WallTok: Supabase (database and sign-in), GitHub Pages (website hosting) and Google (sign-in).

Deleting your data

Email upadesh.ng@gmail.com from the address you sign in with and we will delete your shop, products, orders and account within 30 days.
